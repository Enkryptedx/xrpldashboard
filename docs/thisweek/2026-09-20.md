# This week on xrpldashboard — 2026-09-20

*Weekly Sunday post pairing the loudest XRPL-adjacent news with the on-chain reality — every claim linked to the on-site page or the primary source that carries it.*

## 1. CLARITY Act — cloture on the motion to proceed FAILED

The Senate voted **49–50** on Tuesday 2026-09-15 at 2:19 PM ET (18:19 UTC) to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633, the *Digital Asset Market Clarity Act of 2025*. Sixty votes were required; the motion was rejected. Primary source: [Senate.gov Roll Call vote #234](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00234.htm). The bill remains at the motion-to-proceed stage on the Senate Calendar under General Orders (Cal. No. 423); no floor vote on the bill itself has been scheduled. This was the first floor test since the House passed the bill 294–134 on 2025-07-17 (Roll Call #199). Full timeline, primary-source links, and the "what has NOT happened" list live at [/regulation](https://xrpldashboard.com/regulation) (last verified 2026-09-19).

## 2. SEC Innovation Exemption for tokenized NMS stock — chain-agnostic

On Thursday 2026-09-17 the SEC issued an **"Innovation Exemption"** exempting certain distributed-ledger trading venues from the Exchange Act's "exchange" and "dealer" definitions for the purpose of trading tokenized NMS stock. Primary sources: [SEC Release 2026-90](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment) and [SEC Release No. 34-106402](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/exorders/2026/34-106402.pdf) (PDF). Two facts worth stating plainly: the release is **chain-agnostic** — it does not name any specific blockchain, and eligible venues would choose their own DLT — and it is an SEC **exemptive order paired with a request for comment**, not statute. This is unrelated to H.R. 3633's status; both moved this week, but on independent tracks.

## 3. Chain gap on our own signed-snapshot chain — 2026-09-16 → 2026-09-18 (Mac power outage)

Honesty note: the machine that runs our signing walker lost AC power on Tuesday 2026-09-15 at ~11:15 AM ET and stayed off for approximately 96 hours. As a consequence, our signed-snapshot chain has **no leaves for 2026-09-16, 2026-09-17, or 2026-09-18**. Signing resumed on Saturday 2026-09-19; that day's leaf `previous_root` bridges directly back to 2026-09-15's `chain_root`. **We do not backdate leaves.** A fabricated leaf would contradict the entire signed-snapshot claim, so the gap is on the record. See [/methodology](https://xrpldashboard.com/methodology) "Known gaps." Anchor #7 was delayed by one day for the same reason; it landed as a delayed ceremony on Saturday 2026-09-19 with the outage recorded in [/docs/anchor_history.md](https://github.com/Enkryptedx/xrpldashboard/blob/main/docs/anchor_history.md) as the reason.

## 4. XRPL Token Registry — audited collision pool still at 168

The audited ticker-collision pool from the 2026-09-12 registry audit is unchanged: **168 tokens flagged for ticker collision** on issuers with no gateway or bridge claim; a rolling 10–20 are actively trading at any given time and appear in [the live warnings view](https://xrpldashboard.com/tokens?range=warnings). Every warning renders as a match against an identity-bearing ticker (USDT, BTC, ETH, XLM, LTC, BNB, SOL, RLUSD, plus non-standard-code cases); every one links back to a primary source explaining why. `ticker_collision` is a mechanical statement about issuer provenance, not a verdict on the token or its issuer — definitions and boundaries at [/registry/taxonomy](https://xrpldashboard.com/registry/taxonomy).

## 5. Signed receipts on /check.json — 100% signing rate held

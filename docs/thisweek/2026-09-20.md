# This week on xrpldashboard — 2026-09-20

*Weekly Sunday post pairing the loudest XRPL-adjacent news with the on-chain reality — every claim linked to the on-site page or the primary source that carries it.*

## CLARITY Act: cloture failed

The Senate voted 49–50 on Tuesday, Sept 15, at 2:19 PM ET on cloture for the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633. Sixty votes were required. The bill remains at the motion-to-proceed stage; no further Senate action is scheduled. Source: [Senate.gov Roll Call #234](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00234.htm). Timeline and what has not happened: [/regulation](https://xrpldashboard.com/regulation).

## SEC Innovation Exemption for tokenized stock

On Thursday, Sept 17, the SEC issued an exemption allowing certain distributed-ledger venues to trade tokenized NMS stock. It names no blockchain; venues choose their own. It is an SEC order with a request for comment, not legislation. Sources: [Release 2026-90](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment); [Order 34-106402](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/exorders/2026/34-106402.pdf).

## Our signed chain has a gap: Sept 16–18

The machine that signs our daily snapshot lost power Tuesday, Sept 15, and was off four days. No entries were signed for Sept 16–18; we don't backdate, so Sept 19 links straight to Sept 15. Anchor #7 landed a day late. The site stayed up and live ledger data stayed current; pages fed by that machine showed their last update, and the regulation page missed Tuesday's vote until Saturday. Record: [/methodology, Known gaps](https://xrpldashboard.com/methodology).

## Token Registry

168 tokens remain flagged for ticker collision after the Sept 12 audit; 10–20 are trading at any given time. Live list: [/tokens?range=warnings](https://xrpldashboard.com/tokens?range=warnings). A flag is a statement about issuer provenance, not a verdict on the token. Definitions: [/registry/taxonomy](https://xrpldashboard.com/registry/taxonomy).

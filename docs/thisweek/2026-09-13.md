# This week on xrpldashboard — 2026-09-13

*First edition. Weekly Sunday post pairing the loudest XRPL news with the on-chain reality — every claim linked to the on-site page or the ledger row that carries it.*

## 1. CLARITY Act — cloture vote queued for the week of Sept 15

The US Senate returns from recess on 2026-09-14 with cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 — the *Digital Asset Market Clarity Act of 2025* — waiting for it. Majority Leader John Thune filed the motion on 2026-08-08 before the August recess; the invocation vote is [scheduled for the week of Sept 15](https://xrpldashboard.com/regulation) and needs 60 senators to succeed. This is a procedural gate, not final passage — a `yes` opens Senate floor debate on the bill; a `no` stalls it there. The bill passed the House 294–134 on 2025-07-17 (Roll Call #199) and was reported out of Senate Banking 15–9 on 2026-05-14. The full timeline, primary-source links, and the "what has NOT happened" list live at [/regulation](https://xrpldashboard.com/regulation) (last verified 2026-09-11). Exact cloture-vote day is still "week of 2026-09-15" per Senate scheduling; a Senate.gov roll-call URL will be added to /regulation once the specific day is calendared.

## 2. XRPL Token Registry launched — 18 impostor tickers actively warned

The XRPL Token Registry shipped this week with taxonomy v1.0.0: 12 real categories plus 2 review-status values, all documented at [/registry/taxonomy](https://xrpldashboard.com/registry/taxonomy). The 2026-09-12 signed registry snapshot records the current row counts ([source: /.well-known/registry/2026-09-12.json](https://xrpldashboard.com/.well-known/registry/2026-09-12.json)). After a per-token audit that landed this weekend — every canonical entry cited against the brand's own published source; memecoin name reuse (PEPE, DOGE, SHIB, BONK) excluded on the correct reasoning that memecoins deceive no one about backing; AMM LP-token pool-share hex excluded because it is spec-defined structure, not a scam signal — **[18 tokens](https://xrpldashboard.com/tokens?range=warnings) currently carry an active warning**. Every one is a match against an identity-bearing ticker (USDT, BTC, ETH, XLM, LTC, BNB, SOL, RLUSD, plus three non-standard-code cases) on an issuer with no gateway or bridge claim; every one links back to a primary source explaining why. `ticker_collision` is a mechanical statement about issuer provenance, not a verdict on the token or its issuer — definitions and boundaries at [/registry/taxonomy](https://xrpldashboard.com/registry/taxonomy).

## 3. xrpl_stream data-loss fix + honest undercount disclosure

On 2026-09-07 18:56 UTC we raised the `send_queue_limit` on the local rippled node's subscription stream from the default (100) to 500. The default was quietly kicking the loopback walker off with a "client is too slow" disconnect whenever inbound activity outran the reader — a silent data-loss mode under load, not a hard failure that would page. Post-fix reconciliation against an independent trace puts the historical undercount at **roughly 35%, within a plausible 12–43% band** depending on the sampled window. That range is now disclosed on [/methodology](https://xrpldashboard.com/methodology). Separately, and worth admitting: [/methodology](https://xrpldashboard.com/methodology) itself was offline for approximately 56 hours earlier in the week before this fix landed — an outage of the very page that documents our limitations. It is on the record here rather than smoothed over.

## 5. Signed receipts live on /check.json

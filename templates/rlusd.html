Mastercard credit-card settlement pilot · 2025-11-05
Ripple announced a collaboration with Mastercard, WebBank, and Gemini to use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for stablecoin settlement of fiat card transactions — settling the Gemini Credit Card issued by WebBank. Ripple framed it as the XRPL becoming “the backbone for these and other institutional use cases that are transforming how financial services operate.”
Source: ripple.com press release, 2025-11-05
Aviva Investors × Ripple — exploring fund tokenization on XRPL · 2026-02-11
Aviva Investors, the global asset management business of Aviva plc, and Ripple announced a partnership with the intention of tokenising traditional fund structures on XRPL — Ripple’s first collaboration with an investment management business based in Europe. Aviva Investors’ Chief Distribution Officer, Jill Barber, said: “We’re really delighted to announce our collaboration with Ripple, and we look forward to working closely with the team to explore tokenising solutions.” Ripple’s VP Trading and Markets, Nigel Khakoo, framed it as “tokenisation moving from experimentation to large-scale production,” citing the XRPL’s “built-in compliance tools, near-instant settlement, and native liquidity.” Both parties described a “shared long-term vision” running “over 2026 and beyond.” No tokens have been issued on XRPL yet — this is an announced partnership to develop tokenization, not a deployed product.
Source: ripple.com press release, 2026-02-11
SG-FORGE EURCV deployment · 2026-02-18
Société Générale-FORGE launched its MiCA-compliant euro stablecoin EUR CoinVertible (EURCV) on XRPL — the third chain after Ethereum and Solana at the time of launch. SG-FORGE described it as “supported by Ripple’s custody solution,” with planned use as Ripple-product collateral and trading collateral. Ripple’s MD UK & Europe, Cassie Craddock, called SG-FORGE “a pioneer amongst European institutions when it comes to building out a market-leading crypto-assets offering.” On-chain footprint: issuer rUNa…ut4UL, 10M EURCV currently issued per gateway_balances.
Source: sgforge.com newsroom, 2026-02-18
OKX listing · 2026-04-29
OKX listed RLUSD across 280+ spot pairs (including XRP/RLUSD) and added it as institutional-grade margin collateral for derivatives, including perpetual futures where available. RLUSD has surpassed $1.5B in market capitalization since launching in December 2024.
Source: news.bitcoin.com, 2026-04-29
Cross-border tokenized Treasury redemption · 2026-05-06
Ondo, Kinexys by J.P. Morgan, Mastercard, and Ripple completed the first near real-time cross-border, cross-bank redemption of a tokenized U.S. Treasury fund. Ripple redeemed a portion of its OUSG (Ondo Short-Term U.S. Government Treasuries) holdings on XRPL; the asset leg processed in “under five seconds” on XRPL, while Kinexys settled U.S. dollar proceeds to Ripple’s bank account in Singapore via correspondent banking. Ondo’s Ian De Bode called it “the first time tokenized U.S. Treasuries have settled across borders and banks in near real time and outside traditional banking windows.”
Source: PR Newswire, 2026-05-06
Mastercard on-chain settlement expansion · 2026-06-03
Mastercard announced plans to expand card settlement with “intraday, weekend and holiday” on-chain options using regulated stablecoins. RLUSD on XRPL is among the supported pairings — one of six stablecoins (Circle’s USDC, Paxos’s PYUSD / USDG / USDP, Ripple’s RLUSD, SoFi’s SoFiUSD) across eight chains (Arbitrum, Base, Canton, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Tempo, XRPL). The initial named partners (ARQ, CBW Bank, Cross River, Lead Bank, Nuvei) are not XRPL-anchored institutions. Ripple’s SVP Stablecoins, Jack McDonald, framed RLUSD’s inclusion as reflecting “growing demand for trusted, regulated stablecoins built for real-world financial use cases on public blockchains like the XRP Ledger.”
Source: mastercard.com newsroom, 2026-06-03

Why this exists

Anyone can index the XRP Ledger — the data is public. We’re the only ones doing the editorial layer at scale: hand-curated wallet identities, classified tokens, plain-language event explanations. Your compliance team doesn’t read a hex address — it reads “Bitstamp cold wallet moved $5M to Bitso hot wallet.”

Editorial coverage delivered via API. That’s the product. Webhook alerts, historical depth, custom integrations — those are how it ships.

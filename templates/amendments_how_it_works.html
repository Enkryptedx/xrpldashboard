How the roll call works

Plain-language rules behind the countdown boxes on /amendments.

How the vote works

An amendment needs more than 80% of the trusted validators voting yes. On a trusted-validator list of N, rippled's threshold is floor(0.8 × N), and a majority needs strictly more than that — floor(0.8 × N) + 1 yes votes. Votes are counted at a roll call every 256 ledgers, about every 16.5 minutes. If it holds a majority at every roll call for 14 straight days, it switches on. If a single roll call drops to the 80% threshold or below, the countdown resets to zero, and a new 14 days starts the next time it regains the majority.

Source xrpl.org · Amendments — the voting rule.

Why Batch reset on Sep 25

It was deliberate. A validator operator publicly said he and Ripple switched their votes from yes to no, in coordination with other validators, just enough to reset the countdown — so Batch and its fix (in the new xrpld 3.4.1 release) could switch on together. Our page recorded the exact roll call where it happened.

Why TVL has different statuses: XRP-paired pools with a USD-pegged token (stablecoin or fiat-backed) get an exact TVL. XRP-paired pools whose token has no peg get an estimated TVL — 2× the XRP-side value, since AMM curves balance 50/50. Token-token pools (no XRP side) are tagged non xrp pair and sink to the bottom — we’ll price them once a derived token price feed exists.

Catalog is still expanding. A bootstrap scan walks the ledger by object ID, so the indexed list grows as the scan progresses. The {{ "{:,}".format(indexed_count) }} you see above is the current count — it goes up over time, not down.
